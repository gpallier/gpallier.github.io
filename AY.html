Study group: quasiisometries and cohomologies of nilpotent groups

This is the webpage of the reading seminars on Antonelli and Young's work on ergodic quasiisometries and nilpotent groups.
The study group occurs on some Fridays of the Fall semester 2026 in the Salle Visio of M2 building.
Here you find the paper of Antonelli and Young that we aim to understand. (published in Math. Ann.) Please consult arXiv v3 from July 18.

Tentative program and notes:

All hours given are Central European Time.
  • September 25th, 13:30 - 15:00: General presentation of the paper, Gabriel Pallier.
  • September 25th, 16:00 - 17:00: Blanc's theorem, Marc Bourdon. Notes
  • October 16th, 14:00 - 15:00: Shalom's theorem, Antonio Lopez Neumann.
  • October 16th, 15:30 - 17:00: talk 1: Proof of Proposition 1.2, Fernando Mendez.
  • November 6th, 14:00 - 15:30: talk 2: Construction of the universal pullback, Filippa Lo Biundo.
